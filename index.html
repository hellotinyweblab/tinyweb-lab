How it works

  1. Send the details

    Message me your page link or code, a screenshot and what you want it to look like.

  2. Agree the scope

    I confirm what's included, the price, revisions and delivery date. 50% advance to begin.

  3. Build and deliver

    I do the work, send it for review, make included revisions, and hand over the files once paid in full.

Simple policies

  • Scope first: nothing starts until scope and date are confirmed in writing.
  • Payment: 50% advance, 50% on delivery. On marketplaces, platform escrow is used.
  • Ownership: you own the final files once paid in full.
  • Fix guarantee: if a bug fix doesn't solve the agreed issue, I re-check it free or refund the fix price.
  • Extra work: additional revisions are ₹300 per round. New requests are quoted separately.
  • Concept projects: always labelled as fictional, never presented as client work.
